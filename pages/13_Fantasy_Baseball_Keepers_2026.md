# 2026 Projected Keepers

## Methodology
This analysis identifies the top 5 potential keepers for each team based on their 2025 performance.

**Data Source**: Daily player statistics from the 2025 MLB season.

**Scoring System**: 5x5 Head-to-Head Categories
*   **Batting**: Runs (R), Home Runs (HR), Runs Batted In (RBI), Stolen Bases (SB), On-Base Plus Slugging (OPS)
*   **Pitching**: Earned Run Average (ERA), Walks + Hits per Inning Pitched (WHIP), Strikeouts per 9 Innings (K/9), Quality Starts (QS), Saves + Holds (SVHD)

**Calculation**:
To compare players across different categories, we calculated the **Z-Score** for each stat. A Z-Score measures how many standard deviations a player's stat is from the league average.
*   **Positive Z-Score**: Better than average.
*   **Negative Z-Score**: Worse than average.
*   *Note*: For ERA and WHIP, lower is better, so the Z-Scores are inverted.

The **Total Value (Z)** is the sum of the Z-Scores for all 5 categories. Players are ranked by this total value.
