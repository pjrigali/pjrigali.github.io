## Methodology

This mid-season review evaluates every keeper decision through three lenses. First, **Projection vs. Actual**: using each player's pre-season Blend_Z (a weighted average of 2025 z-score and 2026 projection), we compare actual production to determine whether a keep was correct — rated Exceeding, On Track, Underperforming, or No Data. Second, **IL Impact**: using the MLB transactions file, we calculate days lost and approximate games missed (days × 162/186) for each keeper who spent time on the injured list, then project the statistical production that went uncollected. Third, **Missed Keeps**: for each team we examine the rank-6 through rank-8 alternatives in the pre-season projection file, confirm availability via roster history, and measure the opportunity cost against what was actually kept.

### League-Wide Takeaways

- **Best keeper class overall:** Midnight Muncy's (Robbie) leads with four Exceeding keepers and a team score of 9/10. Juan Soto's return from injury (0.952 OPS in 43 G), Yamamoto's eight quality starts, and three other Exceeding performers give this team the highest verified keeper output in the league, even accounting for Jackson Holliday's 57-day IL absence.

- **Biggest single keep value:** James Wood (Drill ya mama) — kept at round 22 cost for a pre-season ADP of round 4, producing a +18 round surplus. Wood has delivered on that surplus with 53 R, 16 HR, .937 OPS through 62 games, making him the best pure value keeper in the league.

- **Biggest keep mistake:** Jose Ramirez (Shohei Me the Money) — kept at round 1 cost with a Blend_Z of 6.43 and has produced zero statistics to date. Using the first-round pick on an absent player has structurally disadvantaged Mack's team in every category, compounded by the fact that Yordan Alvarez (1.034 OPS, 20 HR) was available to Big Papi at the same round-1 cost and also went unclaimed.

- **Most damaging IL situation:** Honey Nut Chourios (Mike) — Tarik Skubal (44+ days, still out with a left elbow issue) and Garrett Crochet (49+ days, transferred to 60-day IL with left shoulder inflammation) have together cost the team approximately 80 combined games of starting pitcher production. Both were projected as top-5 pitchers. Losing two ace-caliber starters simultaneously is the worst IL outcome for any team this season.

- **Best missed keep across the league:** Yordan Alvarez (available to Big Papi at round 1 cost) is the highest-producing unkept player with a 1.034 OPS and 20 HR through 60 games. Yandy Díaz (available to I Shota the Sheriff at round 19 cost, +6 surplus) is the runner-up at .908 OPS with 11 HR and 38 RBI — a player who would have been the second-best bat on that roster. CJ Abrams (available to Drill ya mama at round 4 cost) is a close third at .901 OPS.

- **Best deviation from projections:** Travis Bazzana (Fresh Prince of Bueh Ler) had the lowest Blend_Z of any kept player (−5.57, ranked 31st across all projected rosters) yet is Exceeding projections with a .833 OPS in 31 games. Brian's decision to keep Bazzana at round 22 — widely regarded as a throwaway pick — has become the most pleasant surprise of the keeper class.
